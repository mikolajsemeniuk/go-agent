# TechCorp Products

## CloudSync Pro
CloudSync Pro is our flagship cloud file synchronization solution.
Price: 49 USD per user per month.
Storage limit: 2 TB per account.
Maximum devices: 10.
Technical support: 24/7 via chat and email.
Available integrations: Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Workspace, Notion.

## DataVault Enterprise
DataVault Enterprise is a backup system for businesses.
Price: 299 USD per 1 TB per month.
Data retention: 7 years.
Encryption: AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit.
Server locations: Warsaw (primary) and Frankfurt (backup).
RTO: 4 hours. RPO: 15 minutes.

## SecureMail Business
SecureMail Business is secure email for companies.
Price: 19 USD per mailbox per month.
Mailbox limit: 50 GB.
Maximum attachment size: 100 MB.
Antivirus: Bitdefender + ClamAV (dual scanning).
Custom domains: unlimited.
